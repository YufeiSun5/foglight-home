# Workshop asset contract

Original editable building modeled for the user's supplied single workshop reference. The reference image is not embedded in any material or file. No imported architecture kit, scan, stock image, character or game asset is used. All texture pixels are original deterministic procedural work, generated by `make_textures.py`; they may be used with this project without third-party attribution requirements.

## Materials and UV

Five texture sets: aged_oak, teal_patina, lime_plaster, limestone, linen. Each contains original 1024² basecolor RGB, scalar roughness, and tangent-space OpenGL (+Y) normal PNGs. Texture nodes are standard Principled BSDF with GLTF-supported sockets; the GLB embeds its textures. Blender source packs texture dependencies.

Nonuniform scale is not used on the mesh objects: primitives are created at their final mesh dimensions before UV assignment. Structural timber UV follows local beam length, rotated with the beam. Grain maps about 2.5 metres along beam length and .6m across grain; random phase offset prevents aligned grain repetition. Roof UV is based on actual arc length, about 2.7 metres per V tile and .75 metres per U tile. Plaster and stone planar world-dimension UV target roughly 2m per tile. Fabric UV follows the draped surface parameterization at about 1.7m × 1.2m per repeat.

Materials: weathered oak / cut edges / deep timber, warm lime plaster, grey limestone / mortar, patinated teal skin / darker folded hems, cream woven canopy, forged iron / oxidation, dark green and muted warm glazing, deep room shadow. Texture roughness is primary, normals subtle. Teal is moderately metallic patinated sheet (metallic .24), with narrow standing seams instead of bulky tiles. Glass is opaque lightly metallic glazing to avoid transparent sorting problems in the pilot.

## Authoring and rendering

- Roof profile is a smooth exponent curve, not an A-frame. Individual curved sheet strips, folded seams, 14cm timber deck and 24cm end verge create editable physical thickness.
- Gables are extruded curved plaster prisms under separate structural trusses. Porch has separate columns, real braces, joists, a door leaf and inset openings.
- Linen has a real 30×22 draped quad mesh, .012m thickness, raised perimeter hems and scalloped valance. It is double-sided.
- Mechanical props, loose furnishings, lights, terrain, plants, final camera and Godot scene belong to other workers. This GLB includes no camera, light or ground plane.
- `foglight_workshop_hero.blend` preserves all individually named meshes and curves plus editable bevel/solidify modifiers. `build_workshop.py` and `make_textures.py` reproduce the asset. Preview images are only model QA, not evidence of final Godot scene fidelity.

## Runtime-optimized alternate

`foglight_workshop_runtime.glb` is a separate rendering-only export: 1 node, 1 combined mesh, 13 material surfaces, explicit triangle topology, all UVs/normals/tangents retained, and all 15 texture images embedded. Opaque surfaces enable backface culling; linen stays double-sided. This cuts the building from 546 drawable mesh objects to 13 material surfaces without losing silhouette or texture resolution. Editable multi-object authoring remains in the original `.blend` and original `foglight_workshop_hero.glb`. `export_runtime.py` recreates this alternate without saving changes to the authoring file. `runtime_gltf_validation.json` records exact structural checks and SHA-256.
